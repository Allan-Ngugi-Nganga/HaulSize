Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At HaulSize, we prioritize your privacy. The app primarily processes your data locally on your device. We do not require you to create an account to use the basic features of the app.

  • Camera & TrueDepth API: We use your device's camera and TrueDepth APIs solely for Augmented Reality (AR) measurements. This visual data is processed in real-time on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymized usage data to help us improve the app's performance and user experience. This data cannot be used to identify you.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information processed by the app is used exclusively for providing the core functionalities—measuring volumes and dimensions. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your measurements, photos, and saved hauls are stored locally on your device or in your personal iCloud account if you choose to enable synchronization. We do not have access to data stored in your iCloud account.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at maverick.develops@gmail.com.
