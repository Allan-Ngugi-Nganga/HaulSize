Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AR measurement?

Our AR measurement relies on the latest LiDAR and ARKit technologies provided by Apple, offering up to 98% accuracy in well-lit environments.

Can I export my measurements?

Yes. You can generate a PDF summary containing all your measurements, photos, and volume calculations directly from the app.

Is my data synced across devices?

If you have iCloud enabled, your data is securely synced across all devices logged into your Apple ID.
